# CertiDraft - Business Model Canvas Documentation

## Executive Overview

CertiDraft is a modern, user-friendly web application designed to streamline the creation, customization, and distribution of digital certificates. Built on Next.js with real-time capabilities and batch processing, the platform serves educational institutions, corporate training programs, and event organizers who need to issue certificates at scale. The platform eliminates complexity in certificate generation by providing an intuitive design experience combined with powerful automation features.

---

## Key Partnerships

CertiDraft relies on several critical external partnerships to deliver its core functionality. **Supabase** serves as the primary backend-as-a-service provider, handling database operations, real-time features, and authentication services that form the foundation of user account management. **SendGrid** provides enterprise-grade email delivery infrastructure, enabling reliable and scalable certificate distribution to end recipients. **Stripe** handles subscription billing and financial transactions, ensuring secure and compliant revenue collection. Additionally, the platform benefits from open-source community partnerships and library contributions through React, Next.js, and component library ecosystems. Integration partners and API consumers who build on top of CertiDraft's infrastructure represent additional partnership opportunities as the platform evolves.

## Key Activities

The platform's core operations center around three primary activity streams. **Certificate Design & Generation** involves maintaining the interactive design builder powered by Fabric.js, enabling users to customize templates with their branding, text, and QR codes. This includes PDF generation using Puppeteer for high-quality output. **Batch Processing & Distribution** manages the complex workflow of processing bulk certificate orders through Redis queue management with BullMQ, ensuring reliable delivery even during high-volume operations. **Account & Subscription Management** encompasses user authentication, subscription tier management, billing administration, and analytics tracking. Administrative activities include template curation for the template gallery, user lifecycle management, and platform monitoring to ensure system reliability and performance.

## Value Propositions

CertiDraft delivers compelling value across multiple dimensions for its target users. **Time Efficiency** allows users to create professional certificates in minutes rather than hours, with pre-built templates reducing design work. **Ease of Use** removes technical barriers—no design or coding expertise required—through an intuitive interface and visual builder. **Batch Automation** enables distribution of hundreds or thousands of certificates automatically via email or download links. **Professional Quality** ensures certificates look polished and branded with customizable design elements. **Verification & Security** includes QR code integration for certificate authenticity verification, adding institutional credibility. **Cost Effectiveness** provides affordable scaling compared to traditional certificate printing and manual distribution methods.

## Customer Relationships

CertiDraft employs a comprehensive relationship strategy tailored to different user segments and tiers. **Tiered Support** provides free tier users with self-service access through in-app guidance, template galleries, and help documentation to enable independent certificate creation, while premium tier subscribers receive priority customer service, faster response times, and dedicated technical assistance. **Community Engagement** maintains user forums, collects feedback, and incorporates user suggestions into product roadmaps. **Proactive Communication** includes real-time notifications about certificate delivery status, analytics insights, and new feature announcements. **Account Management** for enterprise customers includes regular check-ins, usage optimization consultations, and feature customization discussions. **Analytics & Insights** dashboards show certificate distribution metrics, helping users understand their usage patterns and optimize operations.

## Customer Segments

CertiDraft serves three primary and distinct customer segments, each with unique needs and use cases. **Educational Institutions** including schools, universities, online learning platforms, and professional development centers require certificates for course completion, academic achievements, and skill certifications. These organizations value bulk processing, easy distribution to large student cohorts, and professional design templates. **Corporate Training & HR Departments** issue employee training certificates, professional development recognition, compliance documentation, and internal achievement badges. They prioritize security, branding consistency, verification features, and integration with learning management systems. **Event Organizers & Certification Bodies** distribute attendance certificates, professional certifications, industry credentials, and achievement recognition at scale. They value rapid issuance, multiple design options, and verification capabilities to maintain credential authenticity.

## Channels

Users access CertiDraft through multiple, strategically designed channels that optimize discovery, onboarding, and ongoing engagement. **Landing Page** serves as the primary acquisition channel, featuring the hero section for compelling value proposition communication, feature grid showcasing core capabilities, and testimonial sections building social proof. **Template Showcase** drives discovery and engagement by displaying pre-built templates that inspire users and reduce friction for first-time creators. **Pricing Page** presents subscription options transparently, helping potential customers understand value and choose appropriate plans. **Web Application** provides the primary interface for authenticated users to access the certificate builder, project dashboard, and management tools. **Admin Dashboards** enable organizational managers and administrators to oversee user accounts, billing, analytics, and template management. **Email Notifications** maintain engagement through certificate delivery confirmations, usage insights, and platform updates.

## Revenue Streams

CertiDraft generates revenue through a tiered subscription model designed to scale with customer growth across different user needs and usage volumes. **Free Tier** (₱0/month) provides limited access with 5 certificates per month, access to standard templates only, and system watermark on generated certificates, serving as the acquisition funnel and enabling users to experience the platform before committing to paid plans. **Starter Tier** (₱199/month) accommodates growing users with 50 certificates per month, AI-assisted content generation, removal of system watermark, CSV bulk upload capability, and PDF export features, driving conversion from free users. **Pro Tier** (₱599/month) targets active organizations with 300 certificates per month and includes all Starter features plus email automation, priority email delivery, analytics dashboard, and priority customer support. **Enterprise Tier** (₱1,499/month) serves large-scale operations with 1,000 certificates per month and delivers all Pro features plus white-label branding customization, API access for third-party integrations, verification portal for certificate authentication, and dedicated account management. Monthly and annual billing options provide flexibility and incentivize long-term commitments across all paid tiers.

## Key Resources

CertiDraft's competitive advantage rests on several critical technological and human resources. **Technology Infrastructure** includes Next.js 16 for full-stack development, React 19 for dynamic UI components, and Tailwind CSS with Shadcn/UI for consistent design system implementation. **Backend Services** include Supabase for database and real-time features, Redis with BullMQ for distributed job processing, and Puppeteer for server-side rendering. **Certificate Generation Engine** leverages Fabric.js for the interactive design canvas, Puppeteer for high-quality PDF rendering, and QR code libraries for verification. **Intellectual Property** includes proprietary template designs, certificate verification algorithms, and batch processing optimization. **Human Resources** include full-stack developers, DevOps engineers, UI/UX designers, and customer support specialists. **Data Assets** comprise user behavior analytics, template performance metrics, and customer usage patterns that inform product decisions.

## Cost Structure

CertiDraft's cost structure reflects both fixed operational expenses and variable costs that scale with platform growth. **Infrastructure Costs** represent the largest expense category, including Supabase database hosting (₱28,000-112,000/month depending on data volume), Redis instances for job queue management (₱5,600-28,000/month), Next.js application server hosting (₱11,200-56,000/month), and SendGrid email delivery fees (scaling with certificate volume). **Personnel Costs** include salaries for core development team (2-3 full-stack developers), DevOps engineer for infrastructure management, one UI/UX designer, and customer support staff. **Third-Party Services** include payment processor fees (2-3% of revenue), SSL certificates for security, monitoring and analytics tools, and security audit services. **Development & Operations** cover continuous feature development, bug fixes, security patches, and platform maintenance. **Marketing & Customer Acquisition** includes landing page optimization, content marketing, and customer outreach programs. **Compliance & Security** encompasses data protection measures, regular security audits, GDPR compliance, and legal consultation as needed.
